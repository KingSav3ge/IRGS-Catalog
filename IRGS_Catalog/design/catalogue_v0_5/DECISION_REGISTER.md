# IRGS catalogue decision register — v0.5

## Adopted / user-directed

- Scale equivalents: 6 SS = 1 CC; 5 CC = 1 FC; 4 FC = 1 MC; 2 MC = 1 GC. Thus SS 1, CC 6, FC 30, MC 120 and GC 240 SS-equivalents. Equivalence is scope, **not** ownership, bundled purchases, or mandatory embedded story counts.
- Launch portfolio target: 18 Short Stories, 6 Curated Campaigns, 4 Full Campaigns, 2 Major Campaigns and 1 Grand Campaign (31 titles).
- Eighteen Short Stories targeted as free on launch.
- Six initial Short Story premises directly specified by user: Japanese retainer/servant survival; configurable feudal battle; early First Nations / European encounter; being hunted or playing killer; Roman Hadrian's Wall garrison attack; gang initiation.
- MC-01 = **Century of Conflict: The Hundred-Years' War**, **Major Campaign**, not Grand. Persistent historical continuation is compatible with a conflict-specific Major.
- GC-01 = **The World of Kira**, **Grand Campaign**; player-facing intent "anyone, anywhere, anyhow"; pre-existing original lore takes priority over proposals.
- CSS-01 = **Rise of ______**, separate Curated Simulation System category; player-chosen naming, place/time/culture, family/company/grants, establishing household and settlement. Not counted among 31 campaign-tier launch titles.
- IRGS architecture: Core -> Genre -> Setting -> Campaign Profile -> Campaign State; identify reusable contributions upstream through Compare/Merge/Reconcile/Resubmit (CMRR).
- Only Century of Conflict has previous playable *designation*. This package does not run or verify the app, and grants no playability itself.

## Proposed, not adopted

- All other title wording unless explicitly adopted by user.
- Precise historic dates and showcase geographies for Short Stories 7-18, proposed Curated / Full / second Major / future concepts.
- Public preview descriptions, tags, entry roles, featured systems, acceptance tests and proposed app routes.
- The release roadmap, pricing or entitlement for non-Short-Story products, and the public visibility of concept drafts.

## Superseded / not silently reintroduced

- The prior Grand Campaign suggestion "The Age of Realms" was replaced by **The World of Kira**; absent from active catalogue.
- Prior scale conversion 6:8:3:2 replaced by 6:5:4:2.
- "The Roman World" and "The Crowns of Britain" remain future proposals, not launch assignments.
