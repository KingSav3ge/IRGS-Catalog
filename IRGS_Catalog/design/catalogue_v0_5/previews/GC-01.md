## Player-facing synopsis

Inhabit the established fictional World of Kira as an individual or eligible institutional perspective without a mandatory protagonist, allegiance, career, or storyline. Civilizations, settlements, organizations, markets, households, and historic events develop autonomously. Existing source lore—including Akaria, Sadion, Everwood, Kyrinus, Estru, Larius, and TalmahKah—must be preserved and reconciled before being promoted into simulation canon.
