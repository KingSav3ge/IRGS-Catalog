## Player-facing synopsis

The death of a ruler creates questions of succession, rights, offices, and personal loyalty. Relatives, officials, traders, retainers, and ordinary residents continue their lives amid institutional uncertainty. The player may pursue influence, support an institution, protect property, or avoid court politics entirely.
