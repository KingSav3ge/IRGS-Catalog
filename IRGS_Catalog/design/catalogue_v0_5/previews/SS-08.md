> The ocean does not care where you intended to go.

## Player-facing synopsis

An ocean-going vessel suffers navigational disruption, damage, or both during a difficult voyage. Captain, crew, and passengers must contend with weather, provisions, sea conditions, competence, and unequal authority. The ship remains a single persistent asset whose condition and cargo matter.
