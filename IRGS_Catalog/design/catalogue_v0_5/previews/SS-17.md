## Player-facing synopsis

An orbital facility experiences a structural, environmental, or life-support malfunction. Crew and passengers respond through diagnostics, maintenance, emergency procedure, communication, and resource management. Station layout, technical capabilities, limited atmosphere, and component condition remain consistent throughout.
