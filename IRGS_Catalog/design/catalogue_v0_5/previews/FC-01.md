## Player-facing synopsis

Forts, roads, towns, rural estates, markets, and provincial institutions form a functional regional network. Players can inhabit military and civilian lives while goods, people, orders, landholding, and taxes connect different communities. Five substantial community-scale domains participate in the same regional state.
