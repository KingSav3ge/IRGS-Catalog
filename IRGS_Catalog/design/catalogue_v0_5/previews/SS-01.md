## Player-facing synopsis

A retainer and loyal servant become separated from their military force after an ambush. With limited supplies and incomplete information, the pair attempts to reach safety through a region inhabited by villagers, officials, soldiers, and travelers who pursue their own interests. The player chooses either survivor; the other remains autonomous. Returning home is a goal, not a guaranteed or scripted ending.
