## Player-facing synopsis

A living Swahili Indian Ocean trading community connects merchant households, craftworkers, port labor, ships, religious institutions, and visiting traders. Cargo arrivals and departures affect production, wealth, occupations, and local decisions, while residents sustain lives beyond commercial expeditions.
