## Player-facing synopsis

A civilization-scale simulation follows social, economic, and political transformation across Roman and neighboring societies. Historical entry points are grounded, but subsequent player-influenced developments need not reproduce the documented transition. The empire, its communities, and its critics operate as autonomous institutions.
