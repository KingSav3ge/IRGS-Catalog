## Player-facing synopsis

A hospital loses critical services during an active shift. Clinicians, facilities workers, administrators, patients, and support staff continue essential work under changing capacity, equipment, and organizational constraints. Outcomes emerge through triage, technical recovery, communication, and continuity of care.
