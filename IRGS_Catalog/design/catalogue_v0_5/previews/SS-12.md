> One fire can change an entire neighborhood.

## Player-facing synopsis

Fire spreads through a port district containing cargo, residences, shops, workers, and water access. People attempt to rescue others, protect goods, contain flames, and organize responses according to their means and local institutions. Commercial and personal losses persist after the emergency.
