## Player-facing synopsis

A pursuer and an intended victim move through a wooded region containing terrain, weather, infrastructure, and other autonomous people. The player selects either the hunted character or the killer. The scenario revolves around decisions under uncertainty: movement, observation, seeking assistance, pursuit, evasion, and the consequences of encounters.
