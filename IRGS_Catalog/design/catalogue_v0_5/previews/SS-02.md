## Player-facing synopsis

Choose one or two political powers on each side of a historically plausible feudal confrontation. Participate in mobilization, camp routine, military preparation, the battle, and aftermath from any role the society can support, including civilian occupations. The opposing formations, commanders, camp workers, and surrounding communities continue acting independently of the player.
