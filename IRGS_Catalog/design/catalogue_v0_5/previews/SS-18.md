## Player-facing synopsis

A valuable manuscript disappears from a scholarly collection, creating questions of ownership, access, and conflicting testimony. Scholars, scribes, patrons, servants, and local authorities pursue their own responses. Clues exist in established world state and are discovered through investigation, not invented to validate player speculation.
