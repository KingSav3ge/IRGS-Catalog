## Player-facing synopsis

A remote farming community enters a difficult seasonal period. Residents manage crops, fisheries, stored food, animal care, household work, trade, and shared obligations. Multiple linked experiences cover different livelihoods and seasons rather than imposing a sequence of disasters.
