## Player-facing synopsis

A marriage approaches and several households organize their lives around preparations, finances, social expectations, and personal relationships. Guests, relatives, workers, and prospective spouses each have perspectives and independent priorities. The day may proceed peacefully, imperfectly, or change through ordinary consequences.
