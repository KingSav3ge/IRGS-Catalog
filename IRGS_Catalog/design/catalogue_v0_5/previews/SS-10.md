## Player-facing synopsis

A severe flood threatens homes, storehouses, fields, and a river crossing. Residents, officials, ferrymen, and laborers respond within their own knowledge, equipment, and obligations. Evacuation, repairs, rescues, and losses develop from the changing water and infrastructure.
