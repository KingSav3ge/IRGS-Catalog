> Goods, people, and promises must cross the mountains together.

## Player-facing synopsis

A caravan carrying people and goods attempts passage through mountain terrain. Routes, weather, animal health, contracts, local authorities, and market opportunities shape the journey. The player chooses a feasible position within the group while others continue acting independently.
