## Player-facing synopsis

An embassy and its household are embedded within the social, commercial, religious, and political institutions of Istanbul. Correspondence, audiences, employment, servants, interpreters, and personal relationships are tracked through connected situations. The player is free to participate in ordinary life or diplomatic work from a feasible position.
