## Player-facing synopsis

Within the civic, scholarly, craft, and rural networks of a Gupta-era urban center, residents pursue livelihoods, education, institutional standing, personal relationships, and property interests. Connected household and community experiences reveal how the same city can be understood from very different social positions.
