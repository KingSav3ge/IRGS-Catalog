# SS-15 — The Last Train Through

> The journey has stopped. The danger has not.

## Player-facing synopsis

A train becomes stranded or disabled in a remote mountain region. Passengers and railway staff assess mechanical conditions, weather, supplies, and possible routes to safety. Skilled routine maintenance may resolve the incident; failure and danger must arise from actual material conditions.
