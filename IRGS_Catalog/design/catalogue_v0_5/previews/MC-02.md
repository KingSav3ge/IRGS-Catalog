# MC-02 — The Bronze Age Powers

> Empires trade, negotiate, fight, and depend on one another.

**Genres:** Historical, Civilization, Diplomacy, Economy, Military

## Player-facing synopsis

Ancient states and their communities interact through palace and temple institutions, agriculture, tribute, exchange, diplomacy, and military organizations. Four major theaters allow local life alongside international resource and political relationships, without making a known historical collapse an unavoidable scripted endpoint.
