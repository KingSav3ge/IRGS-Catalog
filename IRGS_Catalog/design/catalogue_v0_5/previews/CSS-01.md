## Player-facing synopsis

A player-configurable household and settlement simulation rather than a fixed authored campaign. Choose the period, region, cultures, local institutions, starting household or accompanying settlers, existing rights or absence of a grant, and the name of what rises. Seek permission, establish a livelihood and home, attract households, and potentially develop an enduring community. The system generates separate persistent instances and does not require the player to begin as leader.

## Dependencies and design constraints

- Genre dependencies requiring verification: Historical/Fantasy
- Historical/cultural research: Original-setting validation or specific rules as applicable
- Record ownership: Core remains source of truth for actor, inventory, resource, rights, time and consequence records.
- User agency: Do not invent player speech, thoughts, motives, travel, allegiance, or voluntary decisions.
- Inaccurate universal land grants
- New territory depicted as uninhabited by default
- Automatically assigning player group authority
