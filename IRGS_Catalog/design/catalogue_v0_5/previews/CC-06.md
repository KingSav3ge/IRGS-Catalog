## Player-facing synopsis

Meridian is a connected urban district with functioning homes, jobs, businesses, transport, public services, social communities, and criminal organizations. The player can pursue a routine livelihood, relationships, enterprise, public service, or other feasible path while the population and institutions operate independently.
