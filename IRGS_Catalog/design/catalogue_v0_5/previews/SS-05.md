## Player-facing synopsis

A Roman garrison soldier performs ordinary duties on the northern frontier before an attack by northern forces disrupts the outpost. Actual orders, location, rank, equipment, comrades, and available time determine what the character can do. The attack proceeds as a coherent military event; the player can respond within their position and authority.
