> Two communities meet. Neither knows what comes next.

## Player-facing synopsis

Experience an early encounter between a specific First Nations community and European arrivals, from either side and from multiple possible social positions. Outcomes depend on languages, previous contacts, trade interests, property and land relationships, authority, and what each participant actually knows. Cooperation, cautious coexistence, refusal, and conflict are all feasible; no one outcome is predetermined.
