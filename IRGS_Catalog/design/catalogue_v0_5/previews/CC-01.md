## Player-facing synopsis

In an Egyptian agricultural community, households, fields, marketplaces, temples, and officials operate through interlocking economic and social obligations. Irrigation cycles, food production, taxes, ownership, labor, and family relationships determine what each resident can accomplish. Six linked experience domains share a single persistent population and economy.
