## Player-facing synopsis

A gang associate approaches a ceremony or process that may confer full membership in a criminal organization. Sponsors, peers, leaders, and rivals enter the situation with distinct histories and obligations. The player can respond freely to expectations and consequences rather than follow a forced initiation sequence.
