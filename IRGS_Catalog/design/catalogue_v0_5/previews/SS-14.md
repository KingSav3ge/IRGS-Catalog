## Player-facing synopsis

A local tribunal hears disputes concerning property, agreements, behavior, or obligations. Participants act according to documented procedure and what they believe can be proved. The player may hold office, give evidence, represent interests, or observe proceedings, with any ruling generating subsequent obligations and consequences.
