## Player-facing synopsis

A persistent historical world centered on the Hundred Years’ War and surrounding societies, with selectable historical entry points, careers, affiliations, and independent character pathways. Kingdoms, armies, towns, businesses, families, and institutions operate through actual resources and competing interests. The war is historical context, not a forced player career.
