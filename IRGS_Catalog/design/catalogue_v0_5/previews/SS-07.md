## Player-facing synopsis

A weakened harvest alters the food supply of an ancient town and its nearby farms. Producers, market workers, officials, and households respond using existing stocks, prices, contracts, and social obligations. Characters may trade, work, organize distribution, petition authorities, or try to safeguard dependents as the economy changes.
