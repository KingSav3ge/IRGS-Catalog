## Player-facing synopsis

Communities, agricultural terraces, roads, storage, labor obligations, and imperial officials connect a regional portion of the Andean world. The simulation emphasizes local societies as well as overarching infrastructure and administrative networks, making regional integration visible through actual production and transport.
