# Working brand architecture

The leading working names are **T!DEMARCH Studios** for the developer and
publisher, **WorldWard** for the player-facing platform, and **Fulcrum Engine**
for the underlying simulation technology. IRGS remains the internal development
initiative. The Catalog/Atlas naming decision remains open.

Formal product treatment: **WorldWard — Developed by T!DEMARCH Studios. Powered
by Fulcrum Engine.** A cinematic title may use **WORLDWARD**, preceded by
“T!DEMARCH Studios presents” and followed by “Powered by the Fulcrum Engine.”
The studio's earlier stylized spelling `T!DEM@RCH` and conventional business
name were discussed separately; this document does not establish a registered
legal name or settle alternate wordmarks.

These are leading candidates subject to commercial and trademark clearance.
The reported Worldward/WorldWarD and Fulcrum/Fulqrum overlaps need independent
review; this record does not verify them or determine legal availability. The
architecture does not rename repositories, implementation identifiers or existing
historical/canon records, and it does not approve a public release.
